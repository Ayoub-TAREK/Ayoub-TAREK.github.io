I'm an MSc student in AI & Data Science at IMT Nord Europe. I'm interested in AI for medicine, and above all the brain: neuroimaging and neurological diseases.

  • At GE HealthCare, I worked on locating an ultrasound probe in X-ray images during heart procedures, so cardiologists can see both views on one screen. I first benchmarked foundation models for the task, then used unsupervised domain adaptation so the model holds up on real clinical images it was never trained on.
  • With my team, I won 1st place at Unboxed (Mistral AI x GE HealthCare) with OncoVIA, and built Surgical JEPA, a surgical world model, at Hack the World(s).
  • Before that, at Excelya, I built a fully local RAG system to query internal documents, so no data ever leaves the company.

I'm drawn to AI that has to be both accurate and trustworthy, because the end user is a clinician and the stakes are a patient. I'm looking for a 6-month research internship starting between February and March 2027, before a PhD in medical AI. Feel free to reach out!
