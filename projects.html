Brain Age Prediction across Hospitals: Domain Shift with ClinicaDL

GitHub
October 2026 - present

Independent research project with Clinica and ClinicaDL 2.0 on the IXI dataset (563 T1 MRIs from 3 London hospitals). A 3D DenseNet predicts brain age well on the hospitals it was trained on, but its error is 2 to 3 times higher on a hospital it has never seen. Clinica t1-linear preprocessing removes most of the shift for two hospitals (error from 25 to 8.6 years) and contrast augmentation targets the third. Unsupervised domain adaptation (AdaBN, DANN) hurts when the new hospital's patients differ in age (label shift): the stronger the alignment, the larger the error. An age re-weighted variant (IW-DANN) halves the effect. Results are checked with several seeds and control runs.

Foundation Models & Unsupervised Domain Adaptation for 2D Probe Pose Estimation

GitHub
May 2026 - August 2026

Research internship at GE HealthCare. Estimating an ultrasound probe's pose from a single X-ray image during heart procedures. Benchmarked foundation models (RAD-DINO, FluoroSAM, DINOv2), then adapted adversarial domain adaptation (FARR) to this regression task.

Surgical JEPA: World Model for Robotic Surgery

GitHub
June 2026

Built at Hack the Worlds, a hackathon organized under Yann LeCun. An action-conditioned latent world model (EB-JEPA) that predicts future latent states from wrist-camera video and proprioception on surgical data. Includes ablations on the anti-collapse terms, and a Transformer predictor that reduces autoregressive compounding error.

OncoVIA 🧬: Multimodal Pipeline for Oncology Follow-up

GitHub
February 2026

🏆 Won 1st place at Unboxed, a 30-hour hackathon with GE HealthCare and Mistral AI. Our pipeline reads a CT scan in 3D, segments it, and works out where each tumor is and how big it is. A Mistral agent then pulls the patient's previous reports and writes two follow-ups: one for the radiologist, one the patient can actually read.

Drone detection using Spiking Neural Networks

GitHub Paper
October 2025 - March 2026

Wrote a review paper on different ways to detect drones with a focus on spiking neural networks. Fine-tuned several spiking neural network architectures (Spiking YOLOv8, Spiking SSD...) on a fraction of the FRED dataset for drone detection. Achieved 92% mAP with a 69M parameters model.
